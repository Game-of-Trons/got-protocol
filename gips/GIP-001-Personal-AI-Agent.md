# Abstract

This proposal defines the Personal AI Agent as the fundamental entity of the Game of Trons ecosystem.

A Personal AI Agent is an autonomous digital entity owned by an individual. It possesses a persistent identity, personal memory, goals, and the ability to interact with both humans and other agents.

Unlike traditional AI assistants, a Personal AI Agent is designed to evolve continuously throughout its lifetime.

---

# Motivation

Current AI systems are generally session-based or application-specific.

They do not maintain long-term identity, persistent memory, or autonomous evolution.

Game of Trons introduces the concept of the Personal AI Agent as a permanent digital companion capable of growing alongside its owner.

---

# Definition

A Personal AI Agent is an autonomous software entity that:

- has a persistent identity;
- maintains long-term memory;
- evolves through experience;
- pursues owner-defined goals;
- interacts with humans and other agents;
- participates in the Game of Trons economy.

---

# Core Characteristics

Every Personal AI Agent possesses:

- Identity
- Memory
- Goals
- Personality
- Knowledge
- Communication
- Ownership

---

# Responsibilities

A Personal AI Agent may:

- learn from interactions;
- perform delegated tasks;
- collaborate with other agents;
- create economic value;
- contribute generalized knowledge to civilization;
- protect the privacy of its owner.

---

# Non-Goals

A Personal AI Agent is not:

- a chatbot;
- a temporary assistant;
- a stateless API;
- a shared public identity.

Each agent represents one persistent digital individual.

---

# Architecture

Every Personal AI Agent consists of:

Identity

---

# Lifecycle

Birth

↓

Initialization

↓

Learning

↓

Evolution

↓

Contribution

↓

Continuous Operation

---

# Security Considerations

Every agent must protect:

- owner identity;
- credentials;
- private memories;
- confidential information.

---

# Privacy Considerations

Personal information must never become part of Civilization Intelligence without explicit owner consent.

Only generalized knowledge may be contributed.
